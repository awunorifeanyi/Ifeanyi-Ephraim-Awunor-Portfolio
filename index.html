Research Profile

I am a scholar trained in Theatre and Performance Studies with an interdisciplinary orientation toward historical anthropology, migration studies, ritual performance, and traditional authority in West Africa. My research explores how migrant communities negotiate belonging, legitimacy, and governance through ritual, language, and embodied cultural practices.

Proposed Doctoral Research

Migration, Cultural Hybridity, and Traditional Authority: The Cultural Formation of the Ubulubu Migrant Community in Nigeria

This doctoral project combines ethnographic fieldwork, oral history, and historical analysis to examine how migration produces new cultural formations and how authority and belonging are negotiated through ritual practices such as masquerade, festival performance, and dance.
